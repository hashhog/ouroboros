relocatable deploy pin that start_mainnet.sh uses: promote_mainnet.sh refuses
this node, and tools/reinstall_ouroboros.sh rebuilds sync from source on every

runnable-tree.sha256 plus the currently installed sync .so (informational;

binary. Do not treat it as nimrod-style pin closure.
